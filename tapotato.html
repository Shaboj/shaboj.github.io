Privacy policy

Game-On Creation operates Tapotato: Physics Playground (Android package com.GameonCreation.Tapmaster, previously listed as Tap Master). Contact gameoncreation@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information used by the game

  • Game progress: levels, scores, settings, earned coins, selected appearance and unlocked items are stored on your device. The Android app uses Firebase Authentication, including a guest identifier, and can save game progress to Firebase for account recovery.
  • Optional Google sign-in: when you sign in, Firebase and the game process your Google account identifier, name and email address to identify your account and recover progress and purchases. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles payment details. Our verification service processes purchase tokens, product and order identifiers, purchase time, purchase state and an account binding. The game keeps coin balances, expiry dates, purchased entitlements and refund records. We do not collect card or bank details.
  • Ads and diagnostics: Google AdMob may collect IP addresses (which can estimate approximate location), advertising/app-set or other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and SDK performance or diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not request precise device location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • Support requests: we process information you choose to email us, including your contact address and information needed to resolve a request.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the game, sync progress, verify and restore purchases, prevent duplicate or fraudulent grants, process refunds, show ads and provide support. Firebase/Google Cloud, Google sign-in, Google Play and Google AdMob process data for these functions. Advertising data can be shared with Google's advertising partners according to your choices and applicable settings. We do not sell your account profile to data brokers.

See Google's privacy policy, Firebase privacy information and Google advertising information. Network communication with these services uses encryption in transit. Service providers may process information outside your country.

Adult audience and privacy choices

Tapotato is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The updated app asks for a one-time age-group declaration within the game before starting account services or requesting ads. This self-declaration is stored locally on the device; it is not identity-based age verification. Google sign-in does not provide or verify your age, and we do not request your birthday. Users who select Under 18 cannot continue into the game. Google sign-in and purchases are optional. Ad privacy choices are available in Account when required. Ad content is limited to Google's G rating. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Signing out does not delete your account.

Retention and security

Account profiles, cloud progress and wallet entitlements are kept while the account is active. You can request deletion below. After identity verification, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days and tell you if a delay is necessary. We may retain only records necessary for payment disputes, fraud prevention or legal obligations; we explain any exception and its applicable retention period when handling your request. Data held independently by Google Play or advertising providers follows their policies and applicable law. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Local progress and settings remain on a device until removed through the game's reset option or Android's clear-storage/uninstall controls. Device backups may follow your Android backup settings. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.

Delete your Tapotato account and data

You can request deletion here without reinstalling the app. This is a support-assisted request, not an instant deletion button.

  1. Email gameoncreation@gmail.com with subject Delete my Tapotato account. For a Google-linked account, send it from the email you use in Tapotato. For a guest account, include the account ID shown in the game's Account screen if available.
  2. State that you want your Tapotato account and associated data deleted. You may instead request only your cloud game progress be deleted while keeping your account. Do not send passwords, card numbers or identity documents.
  3. We verify ownership using your account email or account/purchase information where needed. We then delete the Firebase app account, profile, cloud save, wallet balance and associated item entitlements, subject to the limited retention exceptions above. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and confirm completion by email.
Email a deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address and subject into your email service. You can also open Account → Delete account & data inside Tapotato.

Deletion removes access to saved progress, unspent coins and associated items. It does not delete your Google account, automatically refund purchases or remove transaction records held by Google Play. Tapotato coin packs and remove-ads purchases are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. For refunds, use Google Play support. To remove this device's remaining local data, clear Tapotato's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
